1. Information We Collect

Sculptly is designed to work without requiring you to create an account in the current version of the app.

Information you create in the app

Depending on how you use Sculptly, the app may store information such as:

  • Custom workouts you create or edit.
  • Workout history, completion status, challenge progress, and weekly-plan progress.
  • Workout preferences, such as exercise, rest, countdown, sound, haptic, and reminder settings.
  • Favorite exercises or other preferences you choose to save.

Device and app information

Sculptly may receive limited technical information needed to operate, diagnose, secure, and improve the app, such as app version, device type, operating-system version, crash information, and performance data. The exact information available may depend on Apple platform services and any analytics or crash-reporting services enabled in the app.

Information we do not intentionally collect

Sculptly is not designed to collect precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, camera recordings, HealthKit data, or sensitive medical information unless a future feature clearly asks for access and you choose to grant permission.

Sculptly is a general fitness and wellness app. It is not intended to provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized medical advice.

2. How We Use Information

We use information available to Sculptly only for legitimate app-related purposes, including to:

  • Provide workout, custom-workout, challenge, progress, reminder, and personalization features.
  • Remember your preferences and locally saved app state.
  • Restore or confirm access to premium features.
  • Maintain app reliability, troubleshoot errors, and improve performance.
  • Respond to support requests or feedback that you voluntarily submit.
  • Protect the app, users, and services from fraud, abuse, or security issues.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Much of Sculptly's workout-related data is intended to be stored locally on your device, including custom workouts, workout progress, challenge state, settings, and downloaded exercise assets.

If Sculptly introduces optional cloud-based features in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate to explain what information is transmitted, where it is stored, and how it is used.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored data unless that data is separately retained by Apple, a backup service, or another service you have chosen to use.

4. Third-Party Services

Sculptly may rely on third-party or platform services to provide certain functionality. These services may process limited information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Apple: App distribution, TestFlight, in-app purchases, subscriptions, and related platform functionality.
  • Apple-hosted asset delivery: Exercise videos or other app assets may be delivered through Apple-hosted asset services.
  • Analytics or crash reporting: If enabled, technical diagnostics may be processed by the applicable service provider.

We encourage you to review Apple's privacy information and the privacy notices of any third-party service you interact with through the app.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Sculptly does not receive your full payment-card information. Apple may provide Sculptly with transaction or entitlement information needed to determine whether premium features are available to you.

Subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and payment-method management are handled through your Apple ID and App Store account.

6. Notifications and Workout Reminders

Sculptly may ask for permission to send local notifications, such as workout reminders. Notifications are optional. You can disable or change notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Reminder preferences, such as selected days and times, may be stored locally on your device so the app can schedule reminders you request.

7. Children's Privacy

Sculptly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored workout and preference data generally remains on your device until you delete it through the app, reset relevant app data, or remove the app. Information sent to us for support, diagnostics, security, legal, or business purposes may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information associated with Sculptly. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information that is processed about you, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about certain processing practices.

California residents

California residents may have rights under applicable California privacy laws. Sculptly does not sell personal information and does not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

Because much of Sculptly's workout data is stored locally, you may be able to manage or delete that information directly within the app or by removing the app from your device.

11. International Users

If you use Sculptly outside the United States, information processed by third-party platform or service providers may be handled in other countries. Those countries may have data-protection laws that differ from the laws where you live.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Sculptly's features, technology, legal requirements, or data practices change. When we make material updates, we will revise the effective date above and may provide additional notice where appropriate.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Sculptly's privacy practices, please contact us through the in-app support option or the contact information listed on Sculptly's App Store product page.
